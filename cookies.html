1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make sites work, to remember preferences, and to measure and target advertising.

3. What we do not store

Nothing you enter into the calculator is stored in a cookie or anywhere else. Your height, weight, age, sex, activity level and goal are used to do the arithmetic in your browser and are discarded when you close the page. The only preference we retain is your choice of units, and that stays on your device.

4. Advertising cookies and your choices

This site displays advertising served by Google. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites, which enables Google and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits across the internet.

  • Opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of third-party vendors' cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

5. Affiliate links

Following an Amazon affiliate link from this site may cause Amazon to set its own cookies, governed by Amazon's privacy notice rather than this policy.

6. Managing cookies in your browser

Every major browser lets you view, block or delete cookies through its settings, usually under Privacy. Blocking all cookies may affect how some sites behave; on this site the calculator itself will continue to work, since it does not depend on them.

Questions about this policy can be sent via our contact page. See also our privacy policy.
