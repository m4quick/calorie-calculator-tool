Contact Us

We read everything that arrives, and we aim to reply within a few business days. Factual corrections are prioritised over everything else.

What we can help with

  • Calculation errors. If you believe a result is wrong, tell us what you entered and what you expected. Include your units. This is the most valuable message we receive.
  • Questions about the numbers — which formula we use, why the calculator asks for height and age, or how a target was derived. Much of this is answered on how it works.
  • Factual corrections to our guides, particularly the bariatric and GLP-1 protein pages. Please include a source where you can.
  • Bugs, broken links or display problems. Mention your browser and device so we can reproduce it.
  • Advertising, partnership and press enquiries.

What we cannot help with

We cannot answer personal medical or dietary questions, and we will not try. We employ no doctor, nurse or surgeon, and while a dietitian reviews our nutrition content, they are not available for individual consultations through this site — see our editorial policy. We cannot tell you your correct calorie intake, whether a plan is safe for you, or whether a symptom matters. Those questions need a provider who can see your history.

If you are having a medical emergency, do not email us. Contact your healthcare provider or emergency services immediately.
